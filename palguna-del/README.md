# Palguna Week 1 Delivery Pack

Owner: Palguna
Delivery window: 24-28 August 2026

## Outcome

Turn Akanksh's published Amazon and web observations into a product that tells users how every Novel SKU performs against its mapped competitors and what to do next.

Palguna owns:

- S4 Ad Intelligence
- S7 Review and Voice-of-Customer Intelligence
- S8 Sales and Share Estimation
- S9 Benchmarking Scorecard
- S10 Gap and Action Engine
- S11 Alerting and War Room
- Amazon Ads API and its normalization
- Main overview, cross-module product integration and release verification

## Required user journey

1. Open the overview and see current Novel-versus-competitor performance.
2. Select a Novel SKU and see its mapped competitors.
3. Compare rank, search visibility, sponsored presence, price, listing quality, ratings, reviews and availability.
4. Inspect the evidence behind any score.
5. See important gaps and changes ranked by impact.
6. Assign an action, give it a due date and close it with an outcome.
7. See whether the action improved the metric after 7, 14 and 30 days.

## Scope limits

- Amazon.in is the only marketplace this week.
- Meta, Facebook, Instagram and Google Ads Transparency Center are excluded.
- Competitor Amazon bid, budget, ACOS, sales and revenue are not directly observable.
- Estimated values must display a confidence range and their inputs.
- No BOS/SCM integration or shared authentication this week.
- Alerts remain in-product for V1 unless an already configured delivery channel is available without expanding scope.
